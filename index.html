Currently, I am exploring Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Python programming, and data-driven problem solving with the goal of pursuing Computer Science Engineering in the AI & ML domain.

Beyond academics, cricket has played an important role in shaping my personality. As a student athlete and cricket captain, I have developed leadership, discipline, teamwork, adaptability, and the ability to make decisions under pressure.

My Story

The final over of a close cricket match has always felt similar to solving a difficult coding problem. In both situations, everything comes down to focus, clarity, and making the right decision under pressure. Growing up playing cricket, this way of thinking became natural to me even before I started coding.

I started playing cricket seriously and was given the responsibility of captaining my team at the age of thirteen. Leadership taught me that being a captain is not only about making decisions but also about taking responsibility, supporting teammates, and staying calm during difficult situations.

My interest in computer science began when I started exploring coding out of curiosity. What started as experimentation slowly became structured learning. I built Python-based projects related to cricket analytics — a match win predictor and a player performance analyzer.

I worked with datasets, machine learning techniques, and data analysis tools to understand how Artificial Intelligence can help make better decisions in sports.

The process of improving my models taught me that success depends not only on algorithms but also on patience, problem solving, and continuous improvement.

The pandemic was a challenging phase in my academic journey. Like many students, I struggled with consistency during online learning. However, I rebuilt my discipline and developed a stronger approach towards academics and personal growth.

Coming from Karnataka, my family has always encouraged me to value education, hard work, and continuous learning.

I am particularly interested in Computer Science Engineering with a focus on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning because these fields combine logic, creativity, and innovation.

I hope to study in an environment that challenges me, strengthens my technical abilities, and allows me to build solutions that solve meaningful real-world problems.
